\footnotesize Notes: Each row corresponds to one complete linear probability model, rather than to an individual coefficient. The columns report the unweighted percentages of fitted values strictly below zero, strictly above one, and outside the unit interval in total. Only specifications with controls are included, and heterogeneity specifications by municipal tercile are excluded. Each LPM uses exactly the estimation sample, regressors, survey weights, year fixed effects, and municipality fixed effects of the corresponding Logit model. For the post-period exposure and municipal-characteristic models, controls include age and a male indicator. Controlled triple-difference models additionally include Post interacted with the remaining municipal characteristics. The individual-characteristic rows are multivariate specifications. Pooled exposure is measured using the 1970 or 1980 Census, as indicated.
